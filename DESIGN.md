# PT. JKL AutoCredit - Design Direction & Tokens

Lihat dokumentasi lengkap di: [docs/ui_direction.md](file:///d:/Koding/bca/nuxt-app/docs/ui_direction.md)

## Dials
- **ENERGY**: 1 (Calm, structured, authoritative enterprise financial application)
- **RHYTHM**: 2 (Consistent hierarchy, clear card containers, high readability)
- **MOTION**: 1 (Micro-transitions only, no unnecessary bouncy animations)

## Color Palette Tokens
- Primary Brand: `#1e40af` (Corporate Navy Blue)
- Surface Background: `#f8fafc` (Slate 50)
- Surface Cards & Tables: `#ffffff` (Pure White)
- Subtle Border: `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200)
- Strong Border: `#cbd5e1` (Slate 300)
- Text Primary: `#0f172a` (Slate 900)
- Text Secondary: `#475569` (Slate 600)
- Status Approved: `#047857` / BG: `#ecfdf5`
- Status Pending: `#b45309` / BG: `#fffbeb`
- Status Rejected: `#b91c1c` / BG: `#fef2f2`
- Status Revise: `#0284c7` / BG: `#eff6ff`

## Anti-Slop Principles
1. Strictly no em dashes (`—`) in UI copy.
2. Strictly no generic purple/blue neon gradients or glowing mesh backgrounds.
3. No pill-shaped inputs or cards (structured 6px - 8px radius).
4. Real functional states (Loading, Empty, and Error states).
5. Tabular figures for IDR currency and NIK alignment.
6. Real working controls (keyboard navigable, WCAG AA contrast).
